## Cut and truth rules

- Record the actual application build/OS/interface language in the production log. If an export field or action is absent, do not draw or narrate it as present.
- Keep the captured timeline and exported file tied to the same project version. Do not pre-place a different video to simulate a result.
- The 1920×1080/30 target is conditional on source/timeline and real UI support; `ffprobe` is the evidence of output properties.
- The video output itself stays landscape 16:9 for the lesson player. No posting/upload dialog is shown.
